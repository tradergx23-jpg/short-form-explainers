# Short-Form Explainers — 9:16 Reels

Scripts for my 9:16 vertical explainers for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
Format: 720x1280, built in 10-second parts, voiceover in every part and the final.

## Contents
- `mosquito-bite/` — "What happens when a mosquito bites you?" — 40 seconds, 4 parts. Flat 2D cartoon style.
- `ears-pop/` — "Why your ears pop on a plane" — 30 seconds, 3 parts. Stick-figure cartoon style.

## House style
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, one clear idea per 10-second part.
- Big bold captions, simple diagrams, expressive close-ups.
- Voiceover: deep male voice, fast (140 speed) to grab attention.
